# Account verification FAQ

Every business must be verified before it can receive payouts.

## What do businesses need to provide?

A business registration document, a tax identification number and details of every
beneficial owner who holds 25% or more of the company. Sole proprietors provide a government
ID instead of a registration document.

## How long does verification take?

Most accounts are verified automatically within minutes. Accounts that need a manual review
are usually verified within 1 to 2 business days.

## What happens until verification is complete?

The business can accept payments in live mode, but payouts are paused. Pending funds are
paid out as soon as verification is complete.
