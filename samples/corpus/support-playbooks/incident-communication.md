# Incident communication

Clear, regular updates matter as much as the fix during an incident.

## Timeline

- Post the first status page update within 15 minutes of declaring a Severity 1 incident.
- Update the status page at least every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved, even when
  there is nothing new to say.
- Email affected Enterprise customers directly within 1 hour.
- Publish the post-incident report within 5 business days of resolution.

## Writing updates

Say what is affected, what customers see, what we are doing and when the next update will
come. Do not guess at causes or timelines in public updates.
