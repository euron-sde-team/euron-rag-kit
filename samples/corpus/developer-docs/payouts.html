Payouts

A payout moves money from your Acme Payments balance to your bank account. This page explains when payouts arrive, what they cost and what to do when one fails.

Payout schedule

By default payouts are automatic and daily. In the United States, funds from a payment become available for payout two business days after the payment, on a rolling basis.

New accounts have an initial hold: the first payout is sent 7 days after the first successful payment. You can switch to a weekly or monthly schedule in the dashboard.

Minimum amounts

The minimum payout is $1.00. Smaller balances roll over to the next payout.

Instant Payouts

Instant Payouts send funds to an eligible debit card within 30 minutes, at any time of day, including weekends and holidays. Instant Payouts cost 1% of the payout amount, with a minimum fee of $0.50.

  • Available in the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom
  • Up to $10,000 per Instant Payout
  • Not available during the initial 7-day hold

Failed payouts

When a bank rejects a payout we send the payout.failed webhook event with a failure code. The most common reasons are a closed bank account and an incorrect account number. The funds are returned to your Acme balance within 2 to 5 business days, and automatic payouts pause until you update your bank details.

Payout reconciliation

Each payout lists the payments, refunds and fees it contains. Download the payout reconciliation report from the dashboard to match payouts with your bank statement.
